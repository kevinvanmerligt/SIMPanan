## ✨ Core Features

1. **🎯 Multi-Savings Pockets & Financial Goals**
   - Manage multiple independent savings accounts alongside your Main Savings.
   - Set targets, track milestone percentages, and see how much is needed to complete each dream.
   - Quick deposit, quick withdraw, and inter-pocket balance transfers.

2. **📊 Realtime Daily Balance Hero Card**
   - Automatically computes remaining daily balance (**Income - Expenses**).
   - Live total savings balance counter across all active pockets.
   - Formatted local calendar dates and day indicators.

3. **⚡ Quick Transaction Logging (Income & Expense)**
   - Instant modal sheet with iconic categories and pocket allocation.
   - Fast, responsive numeric keypad; saves to local SQLite database in seconds.

4. **🌓 Adaptive Themes & Color System**
   - System auto-switching, permanent Light mode, or permanent Dark mode.
   - Specially calibrated green palette: darker and deeper in light mode for sharp readability, brighter and vibrant in dark mode for soft glowing clarity.

5. **📈 7-Day Expense Bar Chart**
   - Smooth animated bar chart powered by FlChart with horizontal grid lines.
   - Daily breakdown identifying spending trends and highest spending days.

6. **📸 Profile & Interactive Photo Cropping (Zero Lag)**
   - Capture or select avatar photos from Camera or Gallery.
   - Built-in interactive cropper with rotation and smooth gestures running fluidly up to 144Hz.
   - Customizable name, age, and occupation tags.

7. **🔍 Flexible History & Search by Pocket**
   - Filter transactions (*All*, *Income*, *Expense*, or by specific *Savings Pocket*).
   - Realtime instant search by title, category, or pocket name.
   - Swipe-to-delete action with confirmation.

8. **🥧 Category & Savings Distribution Breakdown**
   - Aggregated total spending with category breakdown and progress meters.
   - Proportional distribution of funds across each savings pocket.

9. **🔒 100% Offline Privacy & Security**
   - Device-local SQLite database (`simpanan.db`).
   - Secure preferences stored with Android Keystore encryption.
   - Zero telemetry, no third-party analytics, no tracking, and no advertisements.
